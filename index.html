How an order runs

You deal with one office in Chennai. We deal with the tannery, the factory and the paperwork.

  1. Enquiry and swatches

    Tell us the article, colour, thickness and target price. We collect swatches from tanneries that make it well and send them to you.

  2. Contract

    When you approve, we issue a contract that fixes selection, quantity, price, delivery and payment for both sides.

  3. Production

    We follow the order at the tannery or factory and keep you informed on progress and delivery dates.

  4. Inspection

    Before anything is packed, we inspect the goods against your approved swatch. Strict, and fair to both sides.

  5. Shipment

    We follow the invoice, packing list and airway bill or bill of lading until the goods are on their way to you.
